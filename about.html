About OBMC

OBMC is a progressive, co-educational school offering Pre-K to Grade 12—an enriching alternative to international schools.

Rooted in the Montessori tradition and embracing innovation, we prepare every child for life through Dr. Maria Montessori’s holistic approach to education.

Our mission is to transform our students into self-developing, productive individuals who are empowered to succeed, lead, and shape an ever-changing world.

Explore our programs and discover the vibrant community that makes OBMC a unique place for a child to grow and develop!
